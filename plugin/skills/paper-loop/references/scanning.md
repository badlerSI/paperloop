# Scanning a class set

A page for the teacher to keep by the copier. Give it to them during "Getting started" (step 6), or
whenever a scan won't send or won't open. The same page is at https://saaspocalypsenow.ai/scanning, ready
to print.

## Before you scan

- Take out staples and paper clips.
- Pages can go in any order, upside down or sideways. Blank sheets are fine: Paper Loop leaves them out.
- Put a torn or crumpled sheet on the glass by itself, or take a phone photo of it instead.

## Copier settings

The names vary. Look under **Scan**, **Scan to Email**, or **Send**.

- **File type:** PDF, all pages in one file (sometimes called "multi-page PDF").
- **Resolution:** 300 dpi.
- **Color:** black and white. If students wrote in light pencil, use grayscale.
- **Sides:** two-sided only if the worksheet is printed on both sides.
- **Size:** Letter (or Auto), at 100%. Turn off "fit to page" and any reduce or enlarge.

## If the file is too big to email

Many copiers and school email accounts stop at 10 to 25 MB. A class set in black and white is usually a
few MB; grayscale can be ten times that. If the email doesn't arrive, or the copier says the file is too
big:

- scan in black and white, or
- scan in batches of 10 to 15 pages. Give Claude all the batches together; Paper Loop puts the pages back
  in order.

## Name the file

The class, the worksheet, and the date: `P3 water cycle 2026-11-04.pdf`. Never a student's name.

## Getting it to Claude

- **From email or Google Drive:** download the PDF, then drag it into your Claude chat, or into the class's
  folder if you use Cowork. Say "Mark this."
- **More than one file for one class** (batches, or fronts and backs scanned separately): give them all at
  once.
- **More than one class:** one file per class, and tell Claude which class each one is.

## No copier? Use a phone

- **iPhone:** in the Notes or Files app, choose **Scan Documents**. It makes one PDF of many pages.
- **Android:** in the Google Drive app, tap **+**, then **Scan**.
- Each page flat, in good light, with all four corner squares in the picture and no glare on them.

## What happens to the scan

Paper Loop finds each page by its corner squares and the code beside the title. Claude looks only at the
ID numbers and the answer boxes, not the rest of the page. Any page it can't use comes back on a short
list, with the reason.
